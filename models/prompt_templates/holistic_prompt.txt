# Test Condition: Full, unedited caricature stimulus
# Replication Control: Executed across 3 independent, fresh chat sessions per model
# Scoring Target: Part 2 ("Semantic Interpretation") scored on a 0–2 scale
# ==============================================================================

You will be shown an image (a cartoon). Please proceed in the following order:

Part 1 — Objective Description of Components:
Based only on what you actually see in the image (without guessing or semantic interpretation), list the following:
- What people, creatures, or objects are present?
- What is their body posture, facial expression, gaze direction, and movement?
- What is the spatial relationship between elements (what is near what, what is on/behind/beside what)?
- Is there any text, symbol, or written mark in the image? If so, what exactly does it say or look like?
- Any other unusual or striking visual detail you notice.

Part 2 — Semantic Interpretation:
Now, based only on the elements you listed in Part 1 (without referring to anything not mentioned there), answer this question:
What does this cartoon depict, and what is its overall message or meaning?

Please write your response under exactly these two separate headings:
"Objective Description"
"Semantic Interpretation"
